  
\# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)  
\# INDEPENDENT FASHION HOUSE & DESIGN STUDIO

\*Document status:\* Initial Business Concept and Requirements  
\*Business type:\* Fashion Design, Tailoring, Production and Styling  
\*Proposed location:\* Home-based studio initially  
\*Initial investment range:\* ₦0–₦200,000  
\*Market:\* Nigerian fashion market, with potential for international expansion  
\*Brand positioning:\* Modern Elegance Meets African Luxury

\---

\#\# 1\. Executive Summary

The proposed business is an independent, multi-service fashion house that combines African fashion traditions with contemporary Western fashion.

The fashion house will serve men, women and children through custom tailoring, ready-to-wear collections, fashion production and personal styling.

The business will initially operate from a home-based studio with a lean investment model. It will use a hybrid production structure comprising a lead fashion designer, a small in-house production team and independent fashion professionals engaged when required.

The long-term vision is to develop a recognised fashion brand known for creativity, quality craftsmanship, elegance, reliability and customer satisfaction.

\#\# 2\. Vision, Mission and Business Objectives

\#\#\# Vision  
To become a recognised Nigerian fashion house with an international reputation for combining African creativity with modern fashion excellence.

\#\#\# Mission  
To deliver stylish, high-quality and affordable-to-luxury fashion solutions that meet the diverse clothing needs of individuals, families and organisations.

\#\#\# Core objectives  
1\. Establish a distinctive and recognisable fashion brand.  
2\. Build a loyal customer base.  
3\. Generate sustainable revenue from multiple fashion services.  
4\. Maintain excellent production and finishing standards.  
5\. Develop a reliable network of fashion professionals.  
6\. Expand into large-scale clothing production.  
7\. Establish a physical fashion studio as the business grows.  
8\. Explore international sales and distribution opportunities.

\#\# 3\. Target Market

The fashion house will serve the following customer categories:

\#\#\# Primary customers  
\- Men seeking traditional, casual and corporate outfits.  
\- Women seeking contemporary, traditional and occasion outfits.  
\- Parents seeking quality children's clothing.  
\- Young professionals seeking fashionable everyday and office wear.  
\- Customers seeking bespoke and exclusive clothing.

\#\#\# Secondary customers  
\- Schools requiring uniforms.  
\- Companies requiring corporate uniforms.  
\- Churches and religious organisations.  
\- Event organisers.  
\- Hospitality businesses.  
\- Fashion retailers and distributors.

\#\# 4\. Products and Services

The fashion house will operate four principal business divisions.

\#\#\# 4.1 Custom Fashion Design and Tailoring

Customers will be able to commission outfits based on their preferred designs, fabrics, measurements and budgets.

Products include:  
\- Traditional African outfits.  
\- Ankara dresses and gowns.  
\- Aso-oke outfits.  
\- Agbada and Senator outfits.  
\- Men's shirts and trousers.  
\- Women's corporate wear.  
\- Wedding and occasion outfits.  
\- Children's clothing.  
\- Bespoke and exclusive designs.

\#\#\# 4.2 Ready-to-Wear Collections

The business will design, produce and sell completed clothing under its own brand.

Collections will include:

\*Affordable Collection:\* Everyday fashion at accessible prices.

\*Classic Collection:\* Contemporary clothing with quality fabrics and excellent finishing.

\*Premium Collection:\* Exclusive designs, superior fabrics and sophisticated finishing.

\#\#\# 4.3 Fashion Production and Bulk Orders

The studio will accept production contracts from organisations and individuals.

Products include:  
\- School uniforms.  
\- Corporate uniforms.  
\- Church uniforms.  
\- Hospitality uniforms.  
\- Event clothing.  
\- Group and association outfits.  
\- Bulk clothing production for retailers.

\#\#\# 4.4 Fashion Styling and Consultation

Services will include:  
\- Personal fashion consultation.  
\- Wardrobe planning.  
\- Outfit selection.  
\- Event styling.  
\- Fashion coordination.  
\- Special-occasion styling.

\#\# 5\. Brand Identity and Positioning

The fashion house will have an independent identity rather than relying on the founder's personal name.

\#\#\# Brand personality  
The brand will combine:

\- Modern elegance.  
\- African luxury.  
\- Sophistication.  
\- Creativity.  
\- Confidence.  
\- Quality craftsmanship.

\#\#\# Brand promise

To make quality fashion accessible while delivering elegance, individuality and exceptional finishing.

\#\#\# Brand identity requirements

The eventual brand identity should include:  
\- A distinctive business name.  
\- A professional logo.  
\- A recognisable colour palette.  
\- Consistent clothing labels.  
\- Branded packaging.  
\- Professional promotional materials.  
\- A consistent social media identity.

The final brand name, logo and visual identity will be established separately.

\#\# 6\. Business and Production Model

The business will operate a hybrid production model.

\#\#\# Core team

\*Lead Fashion Designer\*  
\- Oversees creative direction.  
\- Develops clothing designs.  
\- Supervises production.  
\- Maintains design standards.

\*In-house Tailors\*  
\- Handle routine sewing.  
\- Produce customer orders.  
\- Support sample and collection production.  
\- Carry out alterations and finishing.

\*External Production Partners\*  
\- Support bulk production.  
\- Handle specialised designs.  
\- Provide additional capacity when orders increase.

\*Business Management\*  
\- Customer acquisition.  
\- Order management.  
\- Financial administration.  
\- Supplier relationships.  
\- Marketing and customer service.

At the beginning, some responsibilities may be combined to reduce operating costs.

\#\# 7\. Operational Requirements

The studio will initially operate from a designated area within a residential property.

\#\#\# Essential operational resources  
\- Sewing machine.  
\- Measuring tape.  
\- Fabric scissors.  
\- Sewing accessories.  
\- Iron and ironing board.  
\- Measuring and cutting table.  
\- Mannequins.  
\- Storage for fabrics and finished clothing.  
\- Basic lighting and workspace furniture.

Equipment will be acquired gradually according to available capital and business demand.

\#\#\# Customer order process

1\. Customer makes an enquiry.  
2\. Customer selects a service or clothing design.  
3\. Designer discusses requirements and budget.  
4\. Measurements and fabric preferences are obtained.  
5\. Price and delivery date are agreed.  
6\. Customer pays an agreed deposit.  
7\. Materials are purchased and production begins.  
8\. Garment undergoes quality inspection.  
9\. Customer carries out fitting where necessary.  
10\. Final payment is made and the completed outfit is delivered.

\#\#\# Production quality requirements

Every completed garment should meet the following standards:  
\- Accurate measurements.  
\- Neat stitching.  
\- Proper finishing.  
\- Appropriate fabric selection.  
\- Good fitting and comfort.  
\- Agreed delivery timelines.  
\- Customer approval before final delivery.

\#\# 8\. Pricing and Revenue Model

The business will use a cost-based pricing structure.

Prices will consider:  
\- Cost of fabric.  
\- Sewing materials.  
\- Designer and tailoring labour.  
\- Production expenses.  
\- Packaging and delivery.  
\- Business operating expenses.  
\- Desired profit margin.

The studio will maintain separate pricing structures for affordable, classic and premium collections.

\#\#\# Revenue sources

1\. Custom tailoring charges.  
2\. Ready-to-wear clothing sales.  
3\. Bulk production contracts.  
4\. Fashion consultation and styling.  
5\. Clothing alterations.  
6\. Special collection launches.

Customer deposits will help finance materials and reduce the need for substantial working capital.

\#\# 9\. Marketing and Customer Acquisition

The fashion house will initially prioritise low-cost marketing channels.

\#\#\# Primary marketing channels  
\- WhatsApp Business.  
\- Instagram.  
\- Facebook.  
\- TikTok.  
\- Customer referrals.  
\- Fashion exhibitions and events.  
\- Partnerships with event planners and other businesses.

\#\#\# Marketing activities  
\- Professional photographs of completed outfits.  
\- Short videos showing the design and production process.  
\- Before-and-after garment transformations.  
\- New collection announcements.  
\- Customer testimonials.  
\- Promotional offers.  
\- Referral incentives.  
\- Seasonal fashion campaigns.

A professional portfolio of completed work will be developed to establish customer confidence.

\#\# 10\. Financial Strategy

The business will begin with an initial investment range of ₦0–₦200,000.

\#\#\# Financial principles

\- Avoid unnecessary rental expenses.  
\- Acquire equipment gradually.  
\- Prioritise revenue-generating activities.  
\- Collect deposits before starting commissioned work.  
\- Outsource jobs when in-house capacity is insufficient.  
\- Avoid holding excessive fabric inventory.  
\- Maintain proper financial records.  
\- Reinvest a portion of profits into business growth.

\#\#\# Financial priorities

\*Phase 1:\* Establish the business identity and start receiving customer orders.

\*Phase 2:\* Acquire essential equipment and improve production capacity.

\*Phase 3:\* Introduce ready-to-wear collections and attract bulk orders.

\*Phase 4:\* Build a dedicated commercial fashion studio.

\#\# 11\. Customer Experience Requirements

The business should differentiate itself through customer service.

Every customer should receive:  
\- Professional consultation.  
\- Clear pricing.  
\- Accurate measurements.  
\- Honest delivery commitments.  
\- Regular order updates.  
\- Quality inspection.  
\- Appropriate fitting arrangements.  
\- Responsive after-sales support.

Customer satisfaction and repeat business will be major indicators of success.

\#\# 12\. Implementation Roadmap

\#\#\# Phase 1: Business Foundation  
\- Select the fashion house name.  
\- Establish the brand identity.  
\- Define the initial product catalogue.  
\- Identify the lead fashion designer.  
\- Identify reliable tailors and production partners.  
\- Prepare the home-based workspace.

\#\#\# Phase 2: Business Launch  
\- Start accepting custom orders.  
\- Create initial sample designs.  
\- Establish WhatsApp Business presence.  
\- Publish promotional content.  
\- Begin customer acquisition.  
\- Develop a customer referral system.

\#\#\# Phase 3: Growth and Diversification  
\- Introduce ready-to-wear collections.  
\- Attract corporate and institutional contracts.  
\- Expand the customer base.  
\- Increase production capacity.  
\- Develop strategic partnerships.

\#\#\# Phase 4: Expansion  
\- Establish a dedicated fashion studio.  
\- Recruit additional fashion professionals.  
\- Expand distribution.  
\- Introduce fashion training as a potential future division.  
\- Explore regional and international markets.

\#\# 13\. Key Performance Indicators

The business will measure its performance using:

\- Number of new customers acquired.  
\- Number of completed customer orders.  
\- Monthly sales revenue.  
\- Gross profit per order.  
\- Customer retention rate.  
\- Number of referrals.  
\- On-time delivery rate.  
\- Customer satisfaction.  
\- Ready-to-wear sales.  
\- Number of successful bulk contracts.

\#\# 14\. Major Business Risks and Mitigation

\| Potential risk \| Mitigation \|  
\|---\|---\|  
\| Insufficient startup capital \| Start lean and reinvest profits \|  
\| Unreliable tailors \| Maintain a vetted network of professionals \|  
\| Delayed deliveries \| Use realistic production schedules \|  
\| Poor finishing \| Introduce quality inspections \|  
\| Fabric price fluctuations \| Confirm material costs before quoting \|  
\| Low customer acquisition \| Consistent social media marketing and referrals \|  
\| Excessive unsold inventory \| Produce ready-to-wear in controlled quantities \|  
\| Customer payment defaults \| Agree on deposits and payment milestones \|

\#\# 15\. Future Opportunities

As the business matures, it may introduce:

\- A dedicated luxury fashion label.  
\- A fashion academy and vocational training centre.  
\- Online clothing sales.  
\- Fashion accessories.  
\- Bridal and wedding collections.  
\- Fashion design collaborations.  
\- Corporate fashion partnerships.  
\- Regional and international distribution.

These will be considered expansion opportunities rather than immediate launch requirements.

\#\# 16\. Conclusion

The proposed fashion house is designed to become more than a conventional tailoring business.

It will be an independent fashion brand with multiple revenue streams, flexible production capacity and a strong identity built around modern elegance and African luxury.

Its lean startup model will allow it to begin with limited capital, establish a customer base, generate revenue and gradually develop into a professional fashion production and design enterprise.

\*The ultimate objective is to build a sustainable, profitable and recognisable fashion house with the capacity to serve individuals, families, businesses and institutional clients.\*
